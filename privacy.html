4. How long data is kept

  • Account and game data: for as long as the account exists. After a deletion request, the purge takes place at D+7 (7 calendar days); disconnection and removal from the leaderboards are immediate. See the account deletion page.
  • Idempotency record: automatically deleted beyond 90 days (weekly scheduled purge).
  • Server technical logs: kept in a bounded volume through automatic rotation (at most 3 files of 10 MB per service); the oldest entries are continuously overwritten, which amounts in practice to a few days of activity. Maintenance logs are kept for 8 weeks.
  • Database backups: local daily backup kept 7 days; remote backups kept under a rotation of 2 full and 6 differential backups. Deleted data therefore disappears from backups at the end of that rotation.
  • Proof of deletion: after the purge, all that remains is an anonymous audit line: a one-way cryptographic fingerprint and a date. Since the original identifier is destroyed, that fingerprint cannot be used to re-identify you. It is kept without time limit as evidence that the deletion was carried out.
  • Anti-abuse counters: they live with the account and are deleted with it.

5. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time (for notifications: the Android system permission, or the game settings, category by category).

To exercise them, write to bonamyaxelucas@hotmail.fr quoting your account identifier (in the game: Settings › Account & support › Account identifier › Copy). Without it we cannot link a request to an account: the game knows neither your name nor your e-mail address. We answer within one month at the latest.

No automated decision producing legal effects is taken about you: the anti-abuse counters never trigger an automatic sanction, and any sanction goes through human review.

You may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, 75007 Paris — www.cnil.fr, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

6. Age

SOMBROC is intended for players aged 13 and over. In France, giving valid consent on your own to consent-based processing requires being 15 or over; below that age, a parent or guardian must agree. The game is not designed for children and is not enrolled in app-store family programmes.

7. Security

Traffic between the game and the server is encrypted (TLS/WSS). The server administration console is not exposed on the Internet. Operational secrets are never stored in the game's code. Backups are automated and their restoration is tested periodically.

8. What is NOT active today not active

The following items are planned but not integrated in the current version of the game. None of them collects anything today. This page will be updated, at this same address, before any of them is switched on.

  • Google Play Games sign-in: signing in with a Google Play Games account is not offered yet. Today the game only uses the application identifier described in section 2 and receives no Google player identifier.
  • Rewarded advertising and AdMob: no advertising network is integrated, no advertisement is served, and no advertising identifier is read or transmitted. The game currently contains no advertising component. When rewarded advertising is switched on it will stay optional (never a forced ad screen), a certified consent management platform (UMP SDK) will collect your choice in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and this page will describe the data involved before it goes live.
  • In-app purchases: the payment path (Google Play Billing) is not wired; paid shop items currently answer "unavailable". No purchase token, no order number and no billing data is processed.
  • Product analytics (PostHog): no usage-analytics tool is integrated. No usage event leaves your phone. When one is added it will require your prior consent, refusable and revocable, and refusing will take nothing away from the game.
  • Crash reporting (Sentry): no crash-reporting tool is integrated.
  • Device attestation (Google Play Integrity): not integrated; no device integrity verdict is requested or received.

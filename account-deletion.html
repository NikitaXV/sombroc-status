SOMBROC: Account Deletion

Version 3 (pre-launch) · 5 August 2026 · Version française

Privacy Terms of Use Account Deletion Legal Notice

You may ask for your SOMBROC account and its associated data to be deleted at any time. The request is free and does not require the game to be installed.

1. Request deletion (on the web, without the game)

  1. Send an e-mail to bonamyaxelucas@hotmail.fr with the subject "SOMBROC: account deletion".
  2. Include your account identifier in the message. It is the only piece of information that lets us find your save: the game knows neither your name nor your e-mail address.
    • If you still have the game installed: Settings › Account & support › Account identifier › Copy, then paste it into the e-mail.
    • If you have uninstalled the game and no longer have that identifier, write to us anyway: give the nickname shown in the leaderboards and the approximate date of your last session, and we will look for the account with you. Without something that ties you to an account we cannot proceed, as we would be unable to verify that the account is yours.
  3. We record the request on the server and reply by e-mail to confirm it, then again once the deletion has been carried out.

2. From inside the game (without writing to us)

If you still have the game installed and you are signed in, open Settings › Account & support › Delete my account. The game first shows you what will be erased, asks you to tick that you have understood, then asks for one last confirmation; it then displays the exact date of the erasure. This route has exactly the same effect as the web route above, with no e-mail and no waiting for our reply.

One difference only: the game cannot cancel a request. To withdraw it during the 7 days, write to us (point 5).

3. What happens, and when

StepTimingEffect
Request recordedImmediate All your sessions are disconnected and your account is removed from the public leaderboards (depth, kills, duels).
Safety period7 days Your data still exists, but the account is offline. This delay protects against requests made by mistake, or by someone who had access to your phone. You can cancel during this period (see section 5).
Final purgeAt D+7 The data listed below is erased and the account is deleted. The operation is irreversible.

4. What is erased

  • The account itself and its sign-in identifiers.
  • Your in-game currencies (gold, gems), your bag, your chest, your smelting batches, your equipment, your relics.
  • Your progress: maximum depth reached, experience, daily reward cycle, entitlements bought in the shop, rewarded-placement counters.
  • The ghosts you left in the mine and your play-time clocks.
  • Your entries in the three public leaderboards.
  • Your duel rating and the duel invitations you received.
  • Your notification token and your notification preferences.
  • Your anti-abuse counters and your technical operations record.

What remains after the purge: a single anonymous audit line — a one-way cryptographic fingerprint and a date — proving that the deletion did take place. Since the original identifier is destroyed, that fingerprint cannot be used to re-identify you. Technical database backups may still contain your data until they rotate out (a few weeks at most); they are never consulted outside a restore after an incident. Finally, if fraud was established on the account, the corresponding evidence is kept for as long as the case requires.

5. Cancelling a request

As long as the 7 days have not elapsed, write to the same address with the subject "SOMBROC: cancel account deletion" and your account identifier: the request is cancelled and you get your save back untouched. After that deadline, nothing can be recovered.

6. Other rights

You can also ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected, without deleting your account: see the privacy policy.
